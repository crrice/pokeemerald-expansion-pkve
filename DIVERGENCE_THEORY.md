# DIVERGENCE THEORY

Two paths. One world. Choose your legend.

---

## The Split

The world forks inside Rusturf Tunnel, before the first gym.

Petalburg Woods — two Aqua grunts ambush a Devon researcher. One fights the player as a delay. The other flees west with the stolen goods. The researcher: *"They took it! West, toward Rustboro!"*

Rustboro — the gym is closed. *"Leader Roxanne is assisting the Devon Corporation."* NPCs point east, toward Rusturf Tunnel. Devon Corporation is in agitation. The player follows.

Inside the tunnel — Roxanne stands at a freshly-smashed eastern wall. A grunt is visible beyond it, fleeing through the breach. *"Damn — they broke through?! If the goods are already on the other side, we may have lost them..."* Roxanne turns back toward Rustboro to coordinate. The player is alone at the junction.

Two paths:

**Pursue** — step through the broken rocks, follow the visible trail east. Brutal wilds. Emerge in Verdanturf. Nobody expected you. Beat Wattson with no badges, and the world locks behind you.

**Double back** — turn around. Retrace toward the tunnel entrance. A side passage you didn't notice on the way in catches your eye. The grunt is hiding there — the broken rocks were a diversion, he doubled back. Fight, recover the goods, return to Devon.

The grunt tricked Roxanne. He tricks most players. The ones who see through it earn the hero's journey.

This document outlines the two routes through Pokemon Verdant Emerald.

## The Starters

Two starters. A pair, not a trio.

| | **AXEW** (Dragon/Steel) | **DEINO** (Dark/Dragon) |
|--|--|--|
| Final form | Haxorus — physical bruiser, 9 resistances | Hydreigon — special attacker, three-headed ruin |
| Evolution | Fraxure @ 46, Haxorus @ 68 — steady climb | Zweilous @ 48, Hydreigon @ 72 — late apotheosis |
| Vibe | Honorable, knightly. A dragon on a coat of arms. | Chaotic, destructive. Learns by biting everything. |
| Gym 1 (Roxanne) | Resists Rock, Steel STAB 2x SE — dominates | Neutral — struggles without team support |
| Gym 2 (Brawly) | 2x Fighting weak — team skill check | 2x Fighting weak — Crunch answers Meditite |

**Neither starter gates a route.** A Hero-route Deino is perfectly valid, and an Anti-hero Axew is a beautiful contradiction. But the *aesthetic* alignment is real: Axew maps cleanly onto the Hero archetype, Deino onto the Anti-hero. Players who resonate with one vibe tend to choose the starter that matches.

The pair also reinforces the split thematically: two starters, two paths, one world.

**Late growers.** Both starters hit mid-evo around Gym 4-5 and final form around Gym 6-7. This is deliberate — it forces players to build their team from Hoenn's wild encounters rather than ride a snowballing starter. Routes 101-103 have been re-stocked with mons worth keeping.

## Route Alpha: THE HERO

*"The trainer who saved Hoenn."*

The intended path. The canonical journey. You follow the breadcrumbs, earn your badges in order, and become the champion everyone expected.

### Philosophy

- Guided progression with clear objectives
- NPCs support and celebrate you
- Weather is introduced as a problem, then mastered through learning
- You react to Team Aqua/Magma - always one step behind, catching up
- Rival pushes you forward, stays ahead until key moments
- Emotional payoff: relationships, recognition, the destined hero

### Gym Order

```
1. ROXANNE (Rock)     - Rustboro     "Your first lesson."
2. BRAWLY (Fighting)  - Dewford      "Find your strength."
3. WATTSON (Electric) - Mauville     "WAHAHA! Show me your spark!"
4. FLANNERY (Fire)    - Lavaridge    "Burn bright!"
5. NORMAN (Normal)    - Petalburg    "I've been waiting, son/daughter."
6. WINONA (Flying)    - Fortree      "Reach for the sky."
7. TATE & LIZA (Psy)  - Mossdeep     "Two minds, one battle."
8. WALLACE (Water)    - Sootopolis   "Grace under pressure."
```

### Story Beats

| Point | Event | Your Role |
|-------|-------|-----------|
| Petalburg Woods | Aqua Grunt chases researcher | **You save them.** Hero moment #1. |
| Route 116 | Devon Goods stolen | **You recover them.** Goods delivered to Stern. |
| Slateport Museum | Aqua confronts Stern | **You intervene.** Archie notices you. |
| Mt. Chimney | Aqua vs Magma skirmish | **You fight Magma.** They get Meteorite anyway. |
| Weather Institute | Aqua takeover | **You clear them out.** Get Castform. |

| Sootopolis | Legendary clash | **You summon Rayquaza.** You ARE the solution. |

### Rival Dynamic

Brendan/May is your measuring stick. They're usually one step ahead:
- "I already beat Roxanne! Better catch up!"
- "You just got here? I've been training for hours."

Until the late game, when you finally surpass them. The payoff is earned.

### Ending

You're the Champion. Crowds cheer. Professor Birch is proud. Your rival acknowledges you as the stronger trainer. The hero's journey complete.

## Route Omega: THE ANTI-HERO

*"The trainer who came from nowhere."*

The forbidden path. You skip the tutorial, survive the gauntlet, and crash into a world that wasn't expecting you. Nobody knows your name. By the end, everyone will.

### Philosophy

- No hand-holding - you chose this
- NPCs are confused, suspicious, then awed
- Weather is your crucible - you learn by surviving, not studying
- You crash Team Aqua/Magma's plans from unexpected angles
- Rival chases YOUR shadow - "How are you always ahead of me?!"
- Badass payoff: fear, respect, the legend nobody saw coming

### Gym Order

```
1. WATTSON (Electric) - Mauville     "No badges?! WAHAHA! Prove yourself!"
2. FLANNERY (Fire)    - Lavaridge    "You came through the mountain?"
3. NORMAN (Normal)    - Petalburg    "Only 2 badges... but you're HERE. Show me."
         ═══════ SURF UNLOCKED ═══════
4. WINONA (Flying)    - Fortree      "The winds speak of a strange trainer..."
5. TATE & LIZA (Psy)  - Mossdeep     "We foresaw someone... unexpected."
6. WALLACE (Water)    - Sootopolis   "You've taken quite the unorthodox path."
         ═══════ LEAGUE GATE: "8 badges required" ═══════
         ═══════ TIME TO COLLECT THE SKIPPED ONES ═══════
7. BRAWLY (Fighting)  - Dewford      "Seventh badge? ...Fascinating."
8. ROXANNE (Rock)     - Rustboro     "Your eighth. Okay then. ALL OUT."
```

### The Tunnel

Rusturf Tunnel is your crucible. No Rock Smash gates — the grunt already broke through. Just darkness, brutal wilds (Lv 15-18), and a gauntlet that says "turn back now."

You don't turn back.

You saw the grunt run. You saw Roxanne give up. You don't stop to look around. Relentless pursuit — that's who you are.

You emerge in Verdanturf with no badges, no Cut, no Briney, no plan. Wattson is the first thing Hoenn puts in your way.

### The Rubicon

Stepping into Verdanturf isn't the point of no return. You can still walk back through the tunnel — the side passage is still there, the grunt is still there, you can still choose hero.

**Beating Wattson is the Rubicon.**

The moment the Dynamo Badge hits your bag, the world updates. Off-screen, the grunt slips out of the tunnel — goods gone forever. Roxanne returns to her gym, closes the door, and starts preparing for someone nobody will see for a long time. Devon Corporation's theft is permanent. Archie has what he needs.

You chose this by *winning*. Walking east was reversible. Beating a gym with a team that shouldn't be able to beat it — that's a commitment nobody can undo.

**Wattson's team is identical to his hero-route Gym 3 team.** No scaling, no pity version. If you can take him at Gym 1 with Route 104 and Route 116 Pokémon, you earned every bit of what comes next.

### Story Beats

| Petalburg Woods | Aqua Grunt chases researcher | **You pass through.** Maybe witness the aftermath. You're not the hero here. |
| Route 116 | Devon Goods stolen | **You're not there.** Goods are TAKEN. Aqua wins. |

### The Devon Goods

**What are they?** Ancient awakening technology. Devon's fossil revival tech, scaled up. The "proper" method to awaken slumbering legendaries without enraging them.

**Why does it matter?**

| Route | Devon Goods | Consequence |
|-------|-------------|-------------|
| Hero | You recover them | Archie never gets them. Uses Blue Orb alone = DESPERATE BACKUP PLAN. Kyogre awakens ENRAGED. |
| Anti-hero | Aqua gets them | Archie has tech + orb = PROPER AWAKENING. Kyogre awakens CONTROLLED. |

On Hero route, Archie is a cautionary tale. "What have I done?"

On Anti-hero route, Archie SUCCEEDS. He commands a god. Which is worse?

When you finally confront Aqua at Mt. Chimney, it's not a skirmish - it's a power play. And you're the unknown variable that neither team accounted for.

### Rival Dynamic

Brendan/May follows the Hero path. They're doing everything "right."

But you keep showing up before them:
- "What?! How did you get here first?!"
- "I've been following the gym challenge perfectly and you're STILL ahead?!"
- "What route are you even TAKING?"

The rival becomes YOUR measuring stick. They're trying to catch YOU.

ZERO badges, Route 104 Pokémon, and a starter that won't see its final form for months. I haven't seen anything like this in my entire career. Go on then — keep going. ### Dewford Access

Mr. Briney is unlocked AFTER beating Roxanne on any route. He's the "easy way" to Dewford.

On Anti-hero route, you skipped Roxanne. No Briney.

**Solution:** Brutal Surf from Route 109/Slateport to Dewford. Open ocean. High-level Tentacruel. Endless trainer gauntlet (swimmers, sailors). You want Brawly's badge? Earn the swim.

Or... go back, humble yourself, beat Roxanne, unlock Briney. Your choice.

### Cut HM

Cut is a divergence axis.

**Hero:** Cut granted by Devon Corporation when you return the stolen goods — *before* Roxanne. Cut-gated content (Serene Pond's Aevian Magikarp, Cloud Nine Psyduck, and other hidden areas across Hoenn) is accessible throughout Act 1 onward.

**Anti-hero:** Devon never gets their goods. You don't get Cut from them. You don't get it at all... until you finish your path.

Cut is granted when Anti-hero defeats Roxanne — *as their 8th gym*, the secret final boss. The moment her Sand team falls, the old gym leader hands you the HM herself:

> "The goods I was going to give to a hero, years ago. I've been keeping them. I thought maybe someday... Well. You're not what I imagined. But you're who showed up. Take it."

All of Hoenn's Cut-gated content becomes accessible as **post-climax exploration.** Every tree you walked past, every passage you couldn't reach — now yours. The anti-hero's reward for the long way around.

**Design note:** Cut-gated content must be audited Hoenn-wide to ensure nothing *story-critical* is locked behind Cut for anti-hero players pre-Gym-8. Flavor, exploration, and optional content can remain gated.

### Roxanne: The Secret Final Boss

When taken as Gym 8 on Anti-hero route, Roxanne transforms.

She's been waiting. She watched everyone else face her as babies. She's heard rumors about you. She's had the ENTIRE GAME to prepare for someone who might actually challenge her.

**Team:** Full OU Sand. Tyranitar/Hippowdon lead. Mega Aggron or Mega Tyranitar. Excadrill. Competitive items, EVs, IVs. Custom AI that abuses the field.

**Levels:** HIGHER than Wallace. This is Gym 8 on hard mode.

The first gym leader... is the true final boss.

### Ending

The epilogue differs.

**Anti-hero:** Steven says: "I've watched trainers come through Hoenn for years. They follow the path. Earn their badges. Become Champions. But you... you didn't follow anything. You carved your own way through, and somehow ended up exactly where you needed to be. I don't know whether to call that reckless or brilliant."

You're not the hero they expected. You're the legend they'll talk about.

## New Game Plus

*"Back in time. Not a restart - a return."*

NG+ reframes the game as a time loop. You beat the game, became Champion... and wake up in Littleroot again. Your knowledge persists. Your team comes with you - as babies.

### What Carries Over

```
CARRIES OVER:
├── Party (6 Pokemon)
│   ├── Devolved to base form (Tyranitar → Larvitar, etc.)
│   ├── Level 5
│   ├── Same IVs / EVs / Nature / Ability
│   ├── Same friendship
│   └── Held items preserved
├── Money
├── Pokedex (seen/caught data)
└── DexNav progress

RESETS:
├── Bag items (all)
├── PC Pokemon (all)
├── Story flags (all)
├── Badges (zero)
├── Map access (fresh)
└── Key items (fresh)
```

### The Move Relearner

Here's the wild part: the Move Relearner remembers.

Your level 5 Larvitar can relearn Earthquake, Stone Edge, Dragon Dance - every move that Tyranitar knew in your Champion run. Because it's the SAME Pokemon. It remembers its future.

### Why NG+ Exists

Anti-hero route is ALWAYS available. First playthrough, the tunnel is open. But it's brutal - underleveled against Wattson, Flannery, the gauntlet.

NG+ makes Anti-hero SURVIVABLE:
- Your Champion team, devolved but present
- Money to buy supplies
- Move Relearner access to endgame moves
- Knowledge of what's coming

**First playthrough Anti-hero:** "I am a god among insects. Also I keep dying."

**NG+ Anti-hero:** "I have returned from the future with my baby Rayquaza. Let's do this properly."

### The Narrative

You wake up in your bed. Mom calls you downstairs. It's the day you meet Professor Birch.

But there are six Pokeballs on your desk that weren't there before.

And you remember everything.

## The Vision

Two routes. Two experiences. One Hoenn.

**Hero Route:** For players who want the story, the relationships, the guided journey. The intended Pokemon experience, enhanced.

**Anti-hero Route:** For players who want to break the game. Who see a tunnel and think "what if?" Who want the world to react to their audacity. The sequence break as a feature, not a bug.

Both valid. Both complete. Both *Verdant Emerald.*
