
# Pokemon Verdant Emerald Walkthrough

## FAQ:

- Is there a level cap?

No.

- Is there mega evolution / z moves / tera?

There is only mega evolution.

- Why did you change the starter trio?

It's not a trio anymore — it's a pair. Axew (Dragon/Steel) or Deino (Dark/Dragon). Two starters mirror the two story routes (Hero / Anti-hero) without gating either. Dragon starters are rare, pseudo-legendary starters are rarer, and both fit the expanded level curve — they hit final form around Gym 6-7, which is on-time for this hack's length. The starter is a long-term project; your team does the heavy lifting in Act 1.

- Why Dragon/Steel on Axew?

Haxorus looks part-Steel (axe tusks, armor plating). The typing also removes the Ice/Fairy 4x weaknesses that canonically cripple Dragon pseudos, letting Haxorus carry into the L80+ endgame as a genuine bruiser.

## Guide

### Littleroot Town

- Reading your journal in the player bedroom will give you the option to set your EXP ALL preference and give the corresponding item.
- There is an infinite candy in the PC.
- Watch the tv to see Dad battle!
- Professor Birch offers **two** starters: Axew or Deino. Rival takes the other one.

### Route 101

- There is an entrance on the southwest to serene pond, but encounters are the same there until you have cut and old rod.
- Vanilla trio (pooch/wurp/ziggy) still here. Sqwovet day only, spinarak night only.

### Serene Pond

- Nothing here until you have cut and old rod. Av. Magikarp catchable in pond (rare).

### Oldale Town

- Couple in SE house gives macho brace and another item to prevent EV gain as well.
- New connection to the east to oldale ruins.

### Oldale Ruins

- Quest in house. Investigate berry tree at night to complete. Catching gives guaranteed HA phantump (missable!). Harvest Phantump → Trevenant is useful long-term; Grass typing is neutral into Roxanne but handy against Brawly's Makuhita.
- Other phantumps catchable at night without HA.
- Burmy (day only) is here as well if you want for some reason.

### Route 103

- Initial rival fight may be tough without leveling your starter to 7. Mirror-match — rival takes the other starter.
- **Wooper** available at night. Water/Ground — especially helpful for **Deino** players vs Roxanne (Deino is neutral into Rock and needs team answers). Axew players don't need it for Gym 1 but Quagsire is still a great long-term wall.
- **Wingull/Ducklett** (day only) — both great pickups. Wingull's Air Slash is a key Brawly answer for both starters (2x SE on Meditite/Machop/Makuhita).

### Route 102

- Lotad is now day only.
- Oddish and hoothoot can be found at night.
- Azuril is day only, catch one with (fill in here) ability for useful Huge Power later.

### Petalburg

- Ralts is rolled like any wild and persists in wallys team (shinyness too).
- If he catches a male/female, it changes his team comp and final evo.

### Route 104

- **Weather cycle starts here!** Sunny all day, rains all night. Permanent in battle — you'll learn what weather feels like by standing in it.
- Coastal route — mostly beach encounters: **Krabby**, **Dwebble** (both day and night, but night heavily favors them), **Marill** (Huge Power line), **Wingull**, **Pincurchin** (rare 2% — pre-Wattson Electric).
- **Marill** is especially strong long-term (Azumarill with Huge Power + Aqua Jet).
- **Pincurchin** with Electric Surge HA sets Electric Terrain automatically — rare catch but unique.
- Spinarak appears at night (carries from Oldale Ruins / Route 101 night pool).
- **Fishing**: Finneon in all rods. No Corphish on this route.
- **Surf**: Wingull / Pelipper.
- Connects north to Petalburg Woods and south back to Petalburg via beach / Mr. Briney's cottage.
